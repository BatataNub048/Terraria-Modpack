# Fargo enchantment and Force rebalance

## What was verified

- Profile `TodosRecuperados-Titanico-Desempenho`, Terraria 1.4.4.9, tModLoader 2026.9.3.1. All 71 installed mod hashes matched the lock. A temporary read-only probe compiled with zero errors and warnings and reached `PostSetupRecipes` on an isolated dedicated process. It registered zero recipes and opened no world. The audit source and logs are in the local workspace workbench.
- 183 distinct enchantments and 26 distinct Forces. Item identity is the full mod/item ID. Counts by mod for enchantments/Forces are Souls 67/9, Old DLC 99/14, Crossmod 12/2, Extras 5/1. The active official integration is `FargowiltasCrossmod` 1.2.0.28. `FargowiltasSoulsDLC` 0.6.2 is Extras. Older notes about the separately distributed official Calamity DLC do not describe that Extras binary. Old DLC's Calamity and Thorium flags are enabled in the audited configs.
- 184 enchantment recipe rows. Souls Snow Enchantment has two alternate recipes differing by Ice Boomerang versus Ice Blade. Nine Calamity-themed internal base names occur in both Crossmod and Old DLC, including Aerospec, Daedalus and Wulfrum. They have separate mod IDs and distinct registered ingredient sets. No equivalent duplicate recipe for the same output was found. Keep both IDs while verifying effects and downstream Force references before consolidation.
- 25 Forces require four `CalamityMod/DivineGeode` each. The normal acquisition route is Providence. Force of Cosmos requires Cosmilite Bars from Devourer of Gods and Eridanium from Eridanus instead. Thus changing only stations will leave those gates in place. Special seeds, alternate acquisition, and any overridden drops need a separate check. [Providence](https://calamitymod.wiki.gg/wiki/Providence) · [Cosmilite Bar](https://calamitymod.wiki.gg/wiki/Cosmilite_Bar) · [Eridanus](https://fargosmods.wiki.gg/wiki/Eridanus,_Champion_of_Cosmos)
- No version-matched Fargo Souls, Old DLC, Crossmod, Extras or Magic Storage source checkout was located locally. The audit uses the actual installed recipe registries. The registry reports the recipe registrant. A later edit may come from a different mod. Trace targeted recipe hooks or repeat the audit with the relevant integration disabled in a disposable profile before attributing an edit.

## Reading the data

The CSVs contain exact registered crafting recipes, conditions and accepted groups. Nested graphs expand registered crafting alternatives recursively and keep cycles as references. The dependency column lists all recipe-less reachable leaves, including common materials, alternatives and possible drops. Those leaves are leads for acquisition research. They are not all mandatory requirements. Each recipe requires all its listed tiles. Recipe groups substitute matching ingredient representatives and alternatives are not additive. Boss drops, NPC conditions, fishing, world seeds, special conversions and non-recipe acquisition do not appear in a recipe graph. The progression column records estimates or unresolved entries where a leg of the acquisition chain remains unaudited. Review these before writing batch overrides, particularly for Thorium and items with similar names. Current station gates are separate from ingredient estimates.

## First change set

1. **Timber.** Register a new Pre-Hardmode predecessor using one each of Wood, Boreal Wood, Rich Mahogany, Ebonwood, Shadewood and Palm Wood Enchantments. Verify cross-evil materials and the predecessor station on the same seed/rules. Upgrade with Pearlwood Enchantment in Hardmode. This requires explicitly removing or replacing the current four Divine Geodes. Retain the Force's existing item ID and effects. Implement the predecessor initially as a crafting component unless its partial accessory effects are separately approved. Confirm existing Force drops and alternate loot routes.
2. **Terra and Gale.** Candidate complete early Forces if their actual leaves permit. Terra has a plausible late Pre-Hardmode chain after Hellstone, subject to auxiliary materials and ore variants. Gale has a plausible late Pre-Hardmode Calamity chain. Neither needs an intermediate if all constituent effects unlock in the same phase. The shared Divine Geode surcharge must be addressed separately.
3. **Other Forces.** Earth follows the slowest audited Hardmode ore and weapon tier. Nature, Life, Shadow, Spirit and Will also have later armor, Plantera/Golem, invasion or Old One's Army dependencies; choose the station after verifying those components. Elements follows the latest Calamity armor or weapon in its own Crossmod recipe. Thorium Forces keep their essence requirements pending three-source verification, even when much of the enchantment set arrives sooner. Retain final gates for Cosmos, Eternity and the later Calamity Forces pending audits. Add a predecessor only for a useful, coherent set that is otherwise stranded across a large progression gap.

## Mechanism and tests

Use a small standalone compatibility mod with explicit load ordering. Use `ModSystem.PostAddRecipes` and supported recipe APIs to alter existing recipes by full content IDs. Fingerprint ingredients, stacks, groups, station and conditions, and skip/report unmatched or ambiguous recipes. Preserve alternative recipes and manipulation callbacks. Register a new, namespaced item or station only where required. Snapshot in `PostSetupRecipes` to detect edits made after the override. The audited binaries, campaigns and original lock remain the comparison inputs. [tModLoader recipe API](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_recipe.html)

Build the prototype with the same runtime in an isolated profile. Compare baseline and candidate recipe graphs and assert that only allowlisted recipe signatures differ. Load on dedicated server and client, then check recipe availability just before and after the intended gates, at the placed tile and through Magic Storage. Include exact-count consumption, both evil ingredients, late materials, progression or loot bypasses and a multiplayer craft after reconnect. Recheck the lock hashes. No rebalance code has been applied and gameplay tests have not yet run.
