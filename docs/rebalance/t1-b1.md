Iron and Lead equipment recipes retain their exact respective bars. The existing installed profile already has bidirectional 1:1 iron/lead ore and bar conversions at an Altar via Fargo's Mutant Mod and at a furnace via LuiAFK Reborn. A world that generates only one ore can therefore supply both without adding any recipe, NPC or progression shortcut. The vanilla nested recipes remain present alongside optional mod variants.
