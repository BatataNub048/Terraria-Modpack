## Prioritized Tier 1 shortlist

**Batch 1** — Crier, then Marnite Architect. Only two candidates met the evidence threshold. Both original recipes use the Demon Altar and have no registered recipe condition. Crier uses ordinary critter loot and early shop stock. Marnite requires cavern exploration and gold-tier materials.

## Compatibility and open questions

- Installed Calamity/Fables recipe overrides take precedence over standalone reference recipes. Confirm live-version loot conditions for Pearl Shard and Aerialite before reclassifying. Use full IDs to distinguish active Old DLC recipes from disabled Crossmod recipes with similar names.
- Shadow Scale or Tissue Sample alone does not prove a kill gate. Verify segment drops and world-evil alternatives. Confirm safe Book and Bookcase access before promoting the Thorium healer and Icy recipes.
- No confirmed Tier 2 candidate in this pass. The existing Tier 2 build itself has Dungeon and Goblin dependencies, so a theoretical boss-free late ingredient chain is insufficient by itself.
